# Method: content (public prose)

## Steps

1. Draft directly: active voice, key claim first.
2. Unslop: cut AI words, filler, and cliches per rules below.
3. Fact-check: verify numbers and links against sources.

## Principles

- `minimize-reader-load`: Plain words; no filler.
- `explain-the-number`: Name specific counts and metrics.

## Unslop rules (pstack unslop @0c3333f, ids kept)

- 3 No filler -ing tails ("highlighting", "ensuring").
- 5 Name the source. No "experts believe".
- 7 No AI words: crucial, delve, enhance, foster, garner, intricate, pivotal, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.
- 8 Say "is"/"has", not "serves as"/"boasts".
- 9 No "not just X, but Y".
- 10 No forced groups of three.
- 13 No em dashes. Use a period or comma.
- 16-18 No bold-label lists, Title Case headings, emoji.
- 20, 22 No chatbot lines ("I hope this helps").
- 23-25 Cut filler ("in order to"), hedges, generic endings.
- 26, 31 Plain words: use, help. Not leverage, utilize, flywheel.
- 27 Name the mechanism or number, not a feeling.
- 29-30 Active voice. Replace adverbs with numbers.

## Evidence

- Clean prose passing all unslop rules.
- Fact-check notes for all metrics and links.
