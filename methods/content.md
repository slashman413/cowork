# Method: content (public prose)

Use for text the public reads: articles, landing copy, social posts, emails, SEO fields.

- 5 Name the source. No "experts believe".
- 7 No AI words: additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, foster, garner, intricate, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant, landscape.
- 8 Say "is" or "has", not "serves as", "boasts".
- 9 No "not just X, but Y".
- 10 No forced groups of three.
- 13 No em dashes. Use a period or comma.
- 16-18 No bold-label lists, Title Case headings, or decorative emoji.
- 20, 22 No chatbot or sycophantic lines ("I hope this helps", "Great question").
- 23-25 Cut filler ("in order to"), stacked hedges, and generic endings ("the future looks bright").
- 26, 31 Plain words: use, help, many. Not leverage, utilize, paradigm, north star, flywheel.
- 27 Name the mechanism or number, not a feeling.
- 29-30 Active voice. Replace adverbs with the measured number.
